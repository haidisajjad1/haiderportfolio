About Me

Get to know me!

My name is Haider Sajjad and I'm an Aspiring Engineer located in Ilford, London. I’ve always enjoyed turning ideas into reality, whether that be practical projects like making a more comfortable place to study, technical projects like a Bluetooth remote controlled car, or just things for fun – like a working Iron Man helmet. What I really enjoy most is taking an idea and figuring out how to make it work.

Outside of academics, I have explored engineering through CAD, robotics projects, online work experience and competitions. These experiences have strengthened my problem-solving skills, from tackling mathematical challenges in competitions to overcoming the practical problems in my own projects. I have actively sought opportunities to broaden my understanding of engineering, and I now want to take this further.
